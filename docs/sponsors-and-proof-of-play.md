# Sponsors and proof of play

Many schools show sponsor messages during games. Fieldhouse helps you show them at agreed times and keep a record of what actually aired.

## Add a sponsor

1. Open **Sponsors** in the left menu and press Add sponsor.
2. Enter the name, add a logo or image, and choose how long it shows.
3. Set the rules in plain words: when it appears (timeouts, halftime), how often (no more than once every 8 minutes), and a season limit if you promised one.
4. Press Save rules.

If two sponsors want the same slot, Fieldhouse shows a conflict and uses the priority order you set.

## During the game

On the **Live** screen, the "Next sponsor break" card shows what is due. At a timeout it counts down. Press **Fire** to show it, **Skip** to pass, or **Delay** to wait. Nothing airs unless you press Fire, unless you turn on automatic firing in Settings.

## Proof of play

Every Fire, Skip and Delay is written down with the game, time, sponsor and length. Open **Reports** to filter by sponsor, game or date, and export a CSV file to send to a sponsor.

The report lists what Fieldhouse showed. It does not count viewers. Viewer numbers only appear if you type them in, and they are labeled as typed in.

> **Unverified:** the report reflects what Fieldhouse sent to the graphics. It was checked in a scripted game night with real OBS, not against a real public stream. A PDF export is in the design; CSV is the dependable format.

Sample sponsors in the demo are made-up examples.
