# Streaming rights

**This is not legal advice.** Check before you stream.

Some state athletic associations, conferences and districts limit who may broadcast or stream games, and some tournaments (especially playoffs) have exclusive deals with a media partner. Some require a fee, a form or permission, or forbid recording sponsors' ads in certain ways. Rules differ by state, sport and event.

Fieldhouse does not check any of this. The school is responsible.

## What to do

1. Ask your athletic director or the association's office before the season: may we stream home games? Playoffs? Away games?
2. Ask whether visiting schools or their families have to agree.
3. If students are shown, ask what your district requires about photo and video permission.
4. Keep the answer in writing.

## Music

Arena music, pep band and halftime shows may be copyrighted. Streaming platforms can mute or block a stream that includes it. Fieldhouse plays no music.

## Sponsors

If you air sponsor messages, check that school and association rules allow them on a stream.
